[ ] STEP 1.2: Verify the 3 Agent PEAS definitions:
    1. CommitteeAgent (Role A):
       - P: Maximize individual festival utility U_i minus payments while respecting budget B_i.
       - E: Sealed-bid auction rounds, unknown competitor valuations.
       - A: submit_bid(resource, valuation, budget, round_num), allocate_resource().
       - S: Remaining budget, acquired resources, priority schedule, rounds remaining.
    2. AuctioneerAgent (Role B):
       - P: Maximize allocative efficiency & clearing revenue; zero deficit.
       - E: Sealed bids from registered committees, available resource inventory.
       - A: announce_round(), collect_bids(), resolve_clearing_price(), broadcast_allocation().
       - S: Catalog of available resources, active bidder registry, incoming bids.
    3. DisruptionAgent (Role C):
       - P: Inject controlled, realistic operational stress testing (stochastic shocks).
       - E: Current provisional resource allocations.
       - A: sample_disruption(allocations), trigger_invalidation(resource_id).
       - S: Allocation state matrix, Poisson/Bernoulli arrival probability p.
